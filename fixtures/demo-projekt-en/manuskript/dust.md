titel: Dust
---

In the granary the dust of a hundred years lies on the floorboards. On the door hangs a sign that says NO ENTRY. Paul walks along the row of windows on the third floor with his folding rule and writes every width in his notebook.

A message from Konrad appears on Paul’s phone: “Where are the drawings?” He turns the phone over without opening it.

Eighteen windows on each side. Paul counts them once, then once more. Meanwhile seven new messages come in, four of them from Konrad, one from Nordhaven with the subject “Facade, quick question”.

A security guard shines a torch in his face and asks what he is doing up here. Paul doesn’t look up from the folding rule and only says: “Eighteen on each side.”
