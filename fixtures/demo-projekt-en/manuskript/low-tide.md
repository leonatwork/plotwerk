The fish restaurant is two streets behind the Linden Hof. Vera talks about her workshop, where she restores old organs, Paul is only half listening.

Around eleven he says he has to go to Copenhagen for Ole early tomorrow morning. It isn’t true. Vera nods, and he promises: “I’ll call you!”

* * *

At the quay the tide is out, the boats lie lopsided in the mud. The lamp on the bridge flickers, goes out, comes on again, and he doesn’t move.

An old man stops beside him and asks whether he has lost his way. Paul didn’t hear him coming.

* * *

At home he writes to Vera that there is no appointment in Copenhagen.
