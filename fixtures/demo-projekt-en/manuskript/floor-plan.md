On the long table in the kitchenette stands the model of the old granary, thirty centimetres tall, made of grey cardboard. Paul cuts out the last window with the scalpel. Ole sits on the windowsill next to the oleander and eats an apple.

Paul explains why he builds every building out of cardboard first. You have to be able to hold a house in your hand, he says. On a screen you never see where the light is missing.

He lifts the model and holds it up against the sun. Through the tiny windows, flecks of light fall onto the sink, and for a moment Ole stops chewing.

“And what does Nordhaven say to so many windows?” he asks. Paul doesn’t answer straight away.
