In the drawing studio the plans are already hanging on the glass wall when Paul comes in. Malte has connected the projector, Ole is at the building site today. On the table lies the morning paper, the headline right across the front page: Shipyard hall on the North Quay sold.

Paul sits down on a chair by Karl Hansen, opens a Schwip Schwap and tucks the pencil behind his ear. Malte shows the elevations for Nordhaven.

Konrad comes in last and closes the door behind him. He reports that Mr Kessler wants the whole facade in glass, without a single brick. The requirement comes from the supervisory board.

Twelve million is as good as signed, Konrad says, but only with the glass facade. Paul says he’ll take care of it. Konrad wants to see the new drawings by the end of the month.
