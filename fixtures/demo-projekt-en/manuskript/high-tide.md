titel: High Tide
---

I get out of the Volvo, slam the door and watch it until it has disappeared behind the warehouses.

A glance at the church clock: I’m half an hour early, so I order an Aperol Spritz and only notice when I pay that I’ve left all my pencils at home today.

At the boat hire opposite, a father is negotiating with his daughter, she’s maybe eight, over a red rowing boat. He points at the clouds, she points at the boat, he points at his watch. “Just half an hour, Dad, I’ll row all by myself" I hear across the water – "Half an hour, but if it's wobbly, I'll take the oars" he says, giving in. She cheers so loudly that the gulls fly up from the jetty.

I turn round because someone taps me on the shoulder, and see Lotte in front of me, laughing, her hair wet from the drizzle. I take Her in my arms – "Nice that you're here" I say, while I look for a free table
