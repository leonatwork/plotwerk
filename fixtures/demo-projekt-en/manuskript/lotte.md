titel: Lotte

Paul’s flat smells of turpentine and fresh coffee. By the window stands the old oak drawing table that he bought on Kleinanzeigen two weeks ago. The seller was called Lotte and helped him carry it up the four flights of stairs.

Lotte’s last message glows on his phone: half past seven at the Lindenhof. Since the sale they have written to each other every evening.

He irons the light shirt a second time and rolls the sleeves up to his elbows. Then he takes the key and drives his old Saab 900 to the Lindenhof.

At the bascule bridge the traffic backs up, a sailing ship glides slowly through. Paul texts Lotte that the bridge is up and he’ll be late: “Go ahead and order something, I’ll hurry.”

He switches off the engine and watches the mast until the bridge comes down again.
