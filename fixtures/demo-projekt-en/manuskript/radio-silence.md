titel: Radio Silence
---

Paul sits in the Saab in the car park at the Linden-Hof, the cardboard tube with the glass facades on the passenger seat. He hasn’t slept for two nights.

The radio is running a report about empty granaries changing hands overnight. Paul turns it down. In his coat pocket there is a new postcard, one he didn’t put there.

Only at the fish stall does he turn it over: “Why didn’t you come?” A grey van with the word Kranich on its side pulls up in front of the stall. The man at the fish stall wants to know, as always, whether the shrimps are for Fiete again.

In Paul’s flat the phone rings. The display says “Number withheld”. Paul hears gulls and the slap of water, then the line goes dead.

He looks at the calendar on the fridge. Today is Thursday.
