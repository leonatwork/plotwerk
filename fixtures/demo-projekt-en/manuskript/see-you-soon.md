titel: See You Soon
---

Paul wakes up on a wooden bench in the ferry house, his head against the cold window. The Stranger on the bench opposite is still asleep, wrapped in a yellow raincoat.

He doesn’t know how he got here. Outside, fog lies over the water, somewhere the first ferry sounds its horn.

At the counter he orders an Earl Grey. When he pays, he finds a brass key and a postcard in his coat pocket.

The front of the postcard shows the old lighthouse, on the back it says “See you soon” in blue ink. When Paul turns round, the bench opposite is empty.
